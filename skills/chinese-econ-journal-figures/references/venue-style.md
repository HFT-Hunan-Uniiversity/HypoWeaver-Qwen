# Venue style observations

These rules are synthesis rather than a replica of any publisher template. They were derived from representative empirical articles and issue PDFs available from the journals or an author-affiliated university mirror, reviewed on 2026-09-05.

## Shared article structure

The three venues commonly move from a compact title/author/abstract block into numbered sections. Empirical papers typically use this progression:

1. 问题提出或引言
2. 制度背景、文献述评或理论分析
3. 理论机制与研究假说
4. 研究设计：样本、数据、变量、模型
5. 基准结果与识别诊断
6. 机制、异质性或进一步分析
7. 稳健性检验
8. 结论与政策启示

The introduction normally forms one continuous argument: real-world importance, theoretical tension, prior literature, unresolved gap, identification design, headline findings, and two or three concrete contributions. Avoid workflow labels, internal case IDs, model chatter, and generic claims of novelty.

## Tables

- Use a three-line or lightly ruled table on a white page. Do not use colored headers, shadows, rounded cards, or zebra fills in the manuscript view.
- Put `表 N` and a substantive title above the table.
- Regression columns are numbered `(1)`, `(2)`, etc.; the dependent variable or sample distinction belongs in the header.
- Put the coefficient on the first line and the t statistic or robust standard error in parentheses directly below it.
- Report fixed effects, controls, observations, and adjusted R-squared in the lower rows.
- Explain clustering, significance stars, abbreviations, and data status in a note below the bottom rule.
- Align decimals and use only the precision needed for interpretation.

## Figures

- Use black, dark gray, and light gray, with distinct line styles or markers.
- Keep backgrounds white and gridlines absent or extremely light.
- Put the figure number and title below the plot; place the source and statistical note immediately below the caption.
- For event studies, show point estimates, 95% confidence intervals, a dashed zero line, a policy-time marker, and the omitted reference period.
- For coefficient comparisons, use a horizontal dot-and-whisker layout with a vertical zero line and consistent confidence levels.
- For theory diagrams, use simple boxes and arrows. Every arrow should express a mechanism rather than decoration.
- If a figure contains preloaded, simulated, or reconstructed values, add a visible provenance tag and repeat the qualification in the figure note.
